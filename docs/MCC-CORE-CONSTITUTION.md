# MCC-Core Constitution

This document is an architectural constitution, not marketing material.
It states the invariants MCC-Core exists to enforce. Every principle
below is intended to be expressed as an engineering invariant — see
`docs/CONSTITUTION-INVARIANTS.md` for the mapping from each principle to
enforcement points and automated tests, and `docs/CONSTITUTION-COMPLIANCE.md`
for the evidence.

## The Seven Principles

1. Data remains with its owner.
2. Authority remains with the owner.
3. Execution remains within boundaries.
4. Intelligence proposes; authority verifies; execution enforces.
5. Growth of intelligence does not imply growth of authority.
6. Authority is verifiable, not merely trusted.
7. No plane may usurp the role of another.

## The Three Boundaries

Your data.
Your authority.
Your execution boundaries.

## The Formula

Intelligence proposes.
Authority verifies.
Execution enforces.
Data remains.

## The Canonical Doctrine Line

The model proposes.
MCC-Core decides.
The gate enforces.
The audit chain records.

---

A model can be replaced, upgraded, retrained, or made more capable
without any of the above ceasing to hold. The Constitution does not
describe what MCC-Core does when things go well. It describes what
must remain true even when the intelligence layer is wrong, compromised,
manipulated, or simply mistaken about what it should be allowed to do.

The agent can become smarter.
The authority must remain outside the agent.
